Hi, I'm Chadi Ait Ekioui

Applied researcher building agentic AI, LLMs, and systems for Science.

Currently an MPhil student at the University of Cambridge, I am an AI for Science researcher. I design agentic architectures, fine-tune and evaluate LLMs, and ship open-source research software used in real scientific workflows.

My recent work spans collaborations across leading labs and institutions, including Denario, an open-source multi-agent system developed with the University of Cambridge, Cancer Research UK, and the Flatiron Institute to automate end-to-end scientific discovery, Neurolink at ENS Paris for forecasting emergent neuroscience directions from PubMed, and MMORE at EPFL, a multimodal RAG architecture for clinical decision support.
