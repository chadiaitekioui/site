Hi, I'm Chadi Ait Ekioui

Engineering Student

I am an engineering student at Télécom SudParis, pursuing a Master of Science degree with a focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science. My academic journey has taken me from the rigorous Classe Préparatoire aux Grandes Écoles at Lycée Saint-Louis in Paris to international research experiences at prestigious institutions.

My research experience includes working as a Visiting Student at the Cavendish Laboratory, University of Cambridge, where I developed ContextMaker, a Python package for LLM-ready documentation to accelerate discovery in cosmology. I also contributed to an ICML 2025 article and created Synapses, a platform to query top cosmology Python libraries.

Beyond academics, I serve as Vice President of INTOSPACE, the Aerospace and Astronomy Club at Télécom SudParis (IP Paris), leading participation in national competitions from CNES and Planète Sciences, where we won a prize. I'm also passionate about humanitarian work, having served as a Board Member of the Humanitarian Association at Télécom SudParis.
