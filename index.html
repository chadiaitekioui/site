Hi, I'm Chadi Ait Ekioui

As a student researcher in AI for Science, I am eager to explore the vast potential at the intersection of computer science and scientific discovery.

I am currently an engineering student at Télécom SudParis (IP Paris), pursuing a Master of Science degree with a focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science.

My research experience includes working as a Visiting Student at the Cavendish Laboratory, University of Cambridge, where I worked on CMBAgents, a project to develop a planning and control system for autonomous scientific discovery. I also worked on a RAG pipeline for medical data analysis at EPFL, where I implemented multimodal features and an LLM-as-a-judge approach for pipeline evaluation.

Beyond academics, I serve as Vice President of INTOSPACE, the Aerospace and Astronomy Club at Télécom SudParis, leading participation in national competitions from CNES and Planète Sciences. I'm also passionate about neuroscience and space exploration.
